About Wallawalla

Wallawalla is a proudly South African location-based platform designed to help people discover businesses, services, properties, opportunities and essential information near them.

Our mission is simple: Connect communities by making local discovery easier.

What You Can Do On Wallawalla

  • Search for nearby businesses and services.
  • Find restaurants, hotels, pharmacies, shops and more.
  • Discover rental and sale properties in your area.
  • Promote your business to local customers.
  • List properties and reach interested buyers or tenants.
  • Use real-time location features powered by Google Maps.

For Businesses

Wallawalla helps businesses increase their visibility online, attract nearby customers and showcase products and services to a targeted local audience.

For Property Owners

Property owners, agencies and landlords can advertise rentals, houses for sale, apartments, commercial properties and vacant land, making it easier for people to find suitable properties nearby.

Our Vision

We believe communities grow stronger when local businesses, property owners and customers are connected through a simple, trusted and accessible platform.
