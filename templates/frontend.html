About Wallawalla

Wallawalla is a proudly South African location-based discovery platform that helps people find businesses, properties, cars and jobs near them.

Whether you're looking for a local business, searching for a property, looking for your next car or exploring job opportunities, Wallawalla brings local discovery together in one place.

Our mission is simple: Connect people, businesses and opportunities by making local discovery easier.

What You Can Find on Wallawalla

  • Businesses: Discover nearby businesses, shops, restaurants, services, pharmacies, hotels and more.
  • Properties: Find houses, apartments, land and commercial properties available for sale or rent.
  • Cars: Discover local car dealers and vehicles available for sale near you.
  • Jobs: Find local job opportunities from businesses and organisations in your area.
  • Explore on Map: View businesses, properties, cars and jobs around your selected location using Wallawalla's interactive map.

For Businesses

Wallawalla helps local businesses improve their visibility, reach nearby customers and showcase their products and services to people searching in their area.

For Property Owners and Agents

Property owners, landlords and estate agencies can list houses, apartments, commercial properties and land, helping buyers and tenants discover available properties near them.

For Car Sellers and Dealerships

Car dealerships and sellers can showcase vehicles to people searching for cars in their area, making local vehicle discovery easier and more convenient.

For Employers and Job Seekers

Employers can advertise local job opportunities while job seekers can discover opportunities available near them, helping connect local talent with local employers.

Built Around Location

Wallawalla puts location at the centre of discovery. Select your area and explore businesses, properties, cars and jobs that are relevant to where you are or where you want to search.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a trusted South African platform where people can discover what is available around them and where local businesses, property owners, car sellers and employers can connect with the communities they serve.
